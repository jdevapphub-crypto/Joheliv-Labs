Our story

Joheliv Labs was founded in 2026 in South Africa with a simple belief: technology built locally can compete globally. We started as a small team of developers and designers who were tired of watching great ideas get stuck at the "someday" stage.

Today we build apps, websites, online stores and custom software for clients across South Africa and beyond. Every project we take on gets the same treatment — careful design, clean code and a launch plan that actually works.

The name says it all. A codex is a bound collection of knowledge. We take raw code and turn it into something structured, meaningful and built to last.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers to the things people ask us most.

Home / FAQ

What does Joheliv Labs do?
We build apps, websites, online stores and custom software. We also handle UI/UX design, branding and ongoing support.
Where are you based?
We're a South African company founded in 2026. We work with clients locally and internationally — most of our collaboration happens online.
How much does a website cost?
Our Starter websites begin around R4,500. Business websites with custom design and a CMS start around R12,000. Apps, stores and custom software are quoted per project. See our Pricing page.
How long does a project take?
A simple website can take 1–2 weeks. A business site or small app usually takes 3–6 weeks. Larger custom builds are scoped individually.
Do you offer support after launch?
Yes. Every project includes a support window, and we offer ongoing maintenance plans. See our Support page.
Can you redesign my existing website or app?
Absolutely. We can refresh the design, rebuild the code, improve speed and SEO, or extend what you already have.
How do I get a quote?
Message us on WhatsApp at 073 859 2196, email labs@johelivlabs.co, or use our contact form.
What technologies do you use?
We work with modern web and mobile stacks — JavaScript, React, Node, Flutter, and more — chosen to fit each project. We care more about the right tool than the trendiest one.
Do you work with clients outside South Africa?
Yes. We collaborate remotely with clients worldwide. Time zones are never a problem for us.
How do payments work?
Typically a deposit upfront and the balance on delivery. For larger projects we split payments across milestones. Details are agreed in writing before we start.

Last updated: 1 January 2026

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help websites remember your preferences and understand how people use the site.

2. How we use cookies

We may use cookies to:

  • Keep the website working properly.
  • Remember your preferences.
  • Understand how visitors use the site (analytics).
  • Improve performance and content.

3. Types of cookies we use

  • Essential cookies — needed for the website to function.
  • Analytics cookies — help us understand traffic and usage.
  • Preference cookies — remember your settings.

4. Managing cookies

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings. Disabling some cookies may affect how the website works.

5. Third-party cookies

Some services we use (such as analytics providers) may set their own cookies. We do not control these cookies.
